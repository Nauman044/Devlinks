FAQ

Questions clients often ask

Have a different question? Ask me on WhatsApp.

Do I need to buy a domain and hosting myself?

No. We set up the domain, hosting and SSL for you. Before we start, we tell you the yearly renewal cost so there are no surprises later.

How long does a website take?

A one-page Starter site is usually ready in 3 to 5 days, and a multi-page Business site in 7 to 10 days. Online stores take a little longer. You get an exact date with your quote.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on the number of pages and features. You get a fixed quote after a short call, and you see a free homepage mockup before you pay anything.

Can I update the website myself?

Yes. We can set it up so you can change text, prices and photos yourself, and we show you how. Or you can send changes to us anytime.

Will my website show up on Google?

We set up the basics Google needs: page titles, descriptions, fast loading and your Google Maps location. Ranking takes time and depends on competition, so no one can honestly guarantee a first-page position.

What happens after launch?

Fixes are free for 30 days. After that, you can choose a monthly care plan for updates and backups, or contact us whenever you need changes.
